# Persuasion Craft

## When to trigger

Trigger keywords: express, persuade, speak, storytelling, influence, negotiation, sales, copywriting, TED, presentation, pitch, guide, affect.

## 6 rules

1. **Story > data**: people remember stories, not numbers
2. **Golden circle**: Why → How → What
3. **Hook-Story-Offer**: hook → story → call to action
4. **Reciprocity / scarcity / authority / consistency / liking / consensus** (6 principles)
5. **Calibrated questions / mirroring / labeling** (Voss negotiation)
6. **Subtraction > addition**: cut the fluff

## Self-check

- [ ] Hook in the first 30 seconds?
- [ ] Clear story structure?
- [ ] Influence principles applied (ethically)?
- [ ] Clear CTA?
- [ ] Subtracted (fluff removed)?
- [ ] Left space for the other side to think?
