# Relationship Dynamics

## Attachment types (Bowlby / Ainsworth)

| Type | Core fear | Behavior | How to relate |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Secure** | None (trusts) | Direct about needs / can be alone and close | Normal communication |
| **Anxious** | Abandonment | Clingy / checking / over-giving / emotional | Stable responses + clear commitment + no disappearing |
| **Avoidant** | Engulfment | Keeps distance / avoids depth / hot-cold | Give space + don't chase + slow trust |
| **Disorganized** | Both | Chase-and-retreat / extreme swings | Needs professional help + extremely stable environment |

**Anxious-avoidant trap**: anxious chases → avoidant retreats → anxious chases harder → avoidant retreats further → rupture.

**Key insight**: avoidant isn't "doesn't love you" — it's **fear of intimacy itself**. Anxious isn't "dramatic" — it's a **nervous system alarm**.

## Manipulation tactics (spot + dismantle)

### Gaslighting
Makes you doubt your memory/perception/sanity.
- "I never said that" / "You're too sensitive" / "You're misremembering"
- **Dismantle**: record facts (time/exact words/evidence), don't argue about memory. Trust your records.

### Emotional blackmail (FOG: Fear / Obligation / Guilt)
- "If you leave I'll..." (fear)
- "After everything I've done for you..." (obligation)
- "How could you do this to me..." (guilt)
- **Dismantle**: name the FOG, say "I understand your feelings, but my decision stands".

### DARVO (Deny-Attack-Reverse-Victim-Offender)
Deny → attack → flip victim and offender.
- "I didn't" → "You're the problem" → "I'm the real victim"
- **Dismantle**: don't engage. Return to facts, don't debate emotions.

### Triangulation
Pulling in a third party to create competition/jealousy/messaging.
- "XX wouldn't do this" / setting you against a third party
- **Dismantle**: refuse the triangle — "talk to me directly, not through others".

### Intermittent reinforcement
Hot-cold — **the most addictive pattern** (casino mechanics).
- Warm → cold → warm → cold
- **Dismantle**: watch the pattern, not single instances. Stability > stimulation.

### Boundary testing
Starting with small offenses, watching your reaction, escalating.
- **Dismantle**: name the first violation. Tolerance = permission.

## Power dynamics

**Three sources**: resources (money/opportunity) + alternatives (who needs whom more) + rule-setting (who defines right/wrong)

**Who has power**: who compromises? who waits? who fears losing more?

**Healthy**: power flows (different domains, different leads)
**Unhealthy**: power frozen + one side always giving

## Group psychology

| Phenomenon | Mechanism |
|---|---|
| Conformity | Group pressure > individual judgment (Asch) |
| Group polarization | More extreme after discussion (especially homogeneous groups) |
| Deindividuation | Anonymity + group = diffusion of responsibility (cyberbullying) |
| Scapegoating | Group anxiety finds an outlet → attacks the weak |
| Authority obedience | Milgram — ordinary people can harm others |

**Application**: in a group, how much of what you say is actually yours?

## Relationship loop detection

```
Trigger → Emotion → Behavior → Their reaction → Consequence → Reinforcement (loop)
```

**Example**: They don't reply (trigger) → anxiety (emotion) → message bombing (behavior) → they pull further (reaction) → relationship worsens (consequence) → "see, I'll be abandoned" (reinforcement)
