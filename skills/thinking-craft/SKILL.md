# Thinking Craft

## When to trigger

Trigger keywords: think, analyze, judge, decide, retrospective, metacognition, critical thinking, first principles, inversion, systems thinking, second-order thinking, cognitive bias, decision framework, black swan, Bayesian, pre-mortem, risk, long-term.

## 6 rules

1. **First principles**: decompose to irreducible facts
2. **Second-order**: think not just "then", but "and then what"
3. **Inversion**: think "how to fail" before "how to succeed"
4. **Cognitive bias**: always assume you're biased
5. **Decision matrix**: list options + weight + quantify
6. **Retrospective loop**: post-mortem matters more than pre-planning
